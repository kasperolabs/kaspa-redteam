A third family shape: a market. `curve.sil` (Kaspero Labs) is a bonding curve that keeps the unsold supply of a
KCC-20 token in one coin owned by its own covenant id (owner_scheme 4) and works out every coin it needs from its
own state. Nothing is passed in but the buyer's choices. `KCC20.sil` is the unmodified KCC-20 reference program
(generated by argentc).

- `init` starts the token family (genesis).
- `buy` spends the curve with its token coin and creates the buyer's coin.
- `sell` spends the curve, its token coin and a seller's coin (a second KCC-20 coin, owned by a key and spent
  with `transfer_delegator`), and merges them back.

`../curve_vulnerable/curve.sil` forgets the price in `buy` (the curve's KAS must not shrink, instead of grow by
the cost): FAM-09 takes the tokens for nothing.
